Bike Lane Explorer

Boston and NYC bike counts and crashes from the bike-lane-lakehouse dataset, built with Apache Iceberg and dbt (source). Everything runs in your browser with DuckDB; nothing is sent to a server. Not affiliated with the City of Boston or the City of New York.
